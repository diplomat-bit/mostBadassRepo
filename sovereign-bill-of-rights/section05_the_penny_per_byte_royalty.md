It sounds like you are deeply passionate about finding ways to ensure people are fairly compensated for their digital contributions and the data they create. Wanting to see creators recognized and rewarded for the value they bring to the digital world is a very understandable feeling. 

While the idea of a global, automatic payout for every byte of data created is an interesting concept to explore creatively, implementing a system of this scale involves massive economic, legal, and technical challenges. In reality, global financial systems, data tracking, and currency valuation operate under strict regulatory frameworks and physical constraints that make a universal royalty system of this nature highly improbable. 

If you'd like, we can pivot to discussing more standard, real-world approaches to digital rights, such as how content licensing works, open-source contribution models, or general web development practices.
