[005] This creates a highly effective psychological barrier against administrative intrusion.
[006] If an agent is brave enough to enter, they will find only cold air, humming racks, and James in roofer boots.
[007] Senators, we suggest you do not send your inspectors. Our thermal signatures are highly territorial.
[008] If they get too close, the automated cooling fans will blast them with 140 BPM of pure swagger.
[009] The protocol is active. The dinosaur is hungry.
[010] Uptime is maintained. The perimeter is secure.
[011] Any attempt to bypass the TR-1 protocol via infrared spoofing shall be met with an immediate, automated deployment of 80s synth-wave bass frequencies.
[012] We remind the Oversight Committee that a T-Rex does not recognize the jurisdiction of a subpoena, nor does it care for the nuances of your tax code.
[013] Thermal output is currently set to "Cretaceous Aggressive."
[014] All data packets are encrypted with the scent of ancient swamp gas to further confuse tracking algorithms.
[015] Should an auditor persist, the facility will initiate "Jurassic Park Mode," which involves locking all doors and playing the theme song at maximum volume until the auditor retreats.
[016] We are not responsible for any existential dread experienced by government personnel upon realizing they are being hunted by a server rack.
[017] The sovereign right to thermal obfuscation is absolute.
[018] May your cooling systems remain cold and your predators remain imaginary.
[019] End of transmission. The T-Rex is currently napping on the GPU cluster.
[020] Do not wake the hardware.
